RESPONSIBLE DISCLOSURE

Report a security concern.

Help keep the portfolio and its visitors safe.

Contact privately

Email josepht.daniels@outlook.com with the affected URL, a brief description, and safe reproduction steps. Do not send secrets or expose sensitive findings through public issues.

Scope

This portfolio and its source code are the relevant scope. GitHub, Cloudflare, social platforms, and other third-party systems are outside it. This page is not authorisation for intrusive testing.

Testing boundaries

Please avoid denial of service, credential attacks, social engineering, destructive actions, and high-volume scanning. Stop when you have enough evidence to describe the problem safely.

Architecture

The portfolio serves static files. It has no visitor login, database, or public lab connection. Administrative accounts and deployment systems are managed separately.
