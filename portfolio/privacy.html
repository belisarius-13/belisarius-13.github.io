PRIVACY

A small data footprint.

How this portfolio handles information.

Browsing

This site does not use advertising trackers, embedded social feeds, analytics scripts, or visitor accounts. Its theme preference is stored in your browser’s local storage and can be removed by clearing site data.

Contact and meeting requests

The request form prepares an email draft on your device. Form entries are not submitted to a website server or stored by this site. If you send the draft, your email provider and my email provider process the message. I use correspondence to respond to your enquiry and arrange a conversation.

Hosting and external links

Hosting and DNS providers may process technical request information such as IP addresses and access logs. External sites, including GitHub, LinkedIn, and X, have their own privacy practices.

Questions
